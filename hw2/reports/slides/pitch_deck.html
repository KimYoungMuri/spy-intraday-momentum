Investor pitch · AUM raise

A systematic large-cap equity mandate balancing three stock-selection criteria

Investor need

Systematic large-cap stock selection that does not make one characteristic the sole driver of the book — while remaining fully invested in equities and keeping sector weights close to the eligible universe.

Defined product

  • Long-only U.S. large-cap stocks
  • Equal-weight composite of valuation, profitability, and momentum ranks within sector
  • Sector targets matched to the eligible universe at rebalance
  • Monthly rebalance · next-session-close execution
Conditional ask

Illustrative $10 million AUM for this stock-level mandate — if point-in-time validation and operational readiness clear.

Not working capital. Current exploratory research does not establish readiness to deploy.

Hypothesis: combining different information may reduce dependence on any single selection criterion.

Rationale

Why combine? An economic rationale for combining signals

ValuationV = B / M

Book equity over contemporaneous market cap.

Profitability proxyQ = NIann / B̄

Annual net income over period-matched average book — not full balance-sheet strength.

MomentumM = Pt−21/Pt−252 − 1

Trading-session 12−1 total return.

  • Value and Momentum Everywhere (Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen, Journal of Finance, 2013) documents diversification benefits from combining value and momentum across markets — a reason to investigate combinations beyond a short-sample winner. It does not validate this long-only ranking formula. Source: aqr.com / Journal of Finance.
  • Adding signals ≠ reducing risk with cash: value and profitability change which businesses you own while staying fully invested.
  • Composite Si = (Vi + Qi + Mi) / 3 balances overall scores; a name can offset weak profitability with strong value and momentum.
  • Equal weights: transparent starting choice, not fit to this short sample.
Landscape: SPY; iShares VLUE / QUAL / MTUM (0.15% expense ratio each per iShares fact sheets, mid-2026). Also Fama–French value tradition.
Implementation

Exact rules of the stock mandate

Construction

  • ~Top 500 by market cap; price ≥ $5; full 20-session ADV ≥ $1mm
  • Sector percentile ranks → equal-weight composite → top ~20% / sector
  • Sector weights = eligible-universe cap weights at rebalance
  • Signal at month-end close → trade at next session close

Design notes

  • We balance valuation, profitability, and momentum in selection — we do not claim every holding is cheap, high-profitability, and trending.
  • Sector controls also apply to single-factor comparators in our tests — they do not uniquely distinguish VQM from momentum-only.
  • Analysis uses estimated trading costs of 5 bps per side (proportional approximation).
Supported now: published rules and sector targeting. Not claimed: tax overlay, superior execution, or lower cost than factor ETFs.

Complementarity checks

Do holdings and exposures actually differ?

Averages across 30 rebalances: VQM vs Mom Jaccard 42.3%, weight overlap 59.7%. Mom vs Value weight overlap 9.9%.

Portfolio-weighted average sector percentile ranks for VQM: value 0.50 / profitability 0.64 / momentum 0.80 — momentum-tilted with additional profitability exposure; value near the middle of the ranking universe (~0.50).

Correlations of active returns (sleeve return − SPY daily return). Value–Mom ≈ 0.01; VQM–Mom ≈ 0.74. Not regression residuals.

Three labels do not guarantee three independent return sources. Single sleeves differ materially; the composite still leans on momentum in this sample.

Limitations · economics

What remains open — and $10mm trading

Limitations

  • Short sample; current constituents; restated fundamentals; current sectors
  • Profitability proxy ≠ comprehensive quality
  • Lower vol/DD vs momentum is an observed sample feature — not an isolated momentum-crash study
  • 2026 previously examined; costs are an approximation
A rationale for VQM does not automatically justify charging 75 bps. After-fee Sharpe (~1.09) ≈ universe-cap (~1.08) in this sample.

Milestones before any live allocation

  1. Historical membership + as-reported fundamentals with availability dates
  2. Point-in-time rebuild; re-run A–F and overlap/exposure tests
  3. TCA / trade-participation pack at target AUM
